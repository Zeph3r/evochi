How it works

Walk. Grow. Evolve.

Your Evochi grows from the steps your phone records anyway. There's nothing to log and no workout to plan.

  1. Hatch your egg

    Pick one of six colourways, name your Eggling and choose a daily goal of 5,000, 7,000 or 10,000 steps.

  2. Walk, and it grows

    Steps become XP. A full goal earns 150 XP, with bonuses at 25%, 50% and 100% along the way and up to 20 more for going past it.

  3. Evolve together

    At level 10 with 7 active days your Eggling can become a Juvenile, and at level 25 with 21 active days, an Elder. You choose the moment.

Gentle by design

Rewards for walking. No punishment for resting.

These rules are built into the game, not left to chance.

  • Two rest days every week

    The first two low-step days in any seven days never cost your Evochi vitality.

  • 48 hours to sync

    Watch data can arrive late, so a low day only counts after 48 hours.

  • No data isn't a lazy day

    If your phone didn't record steps, the day shows as pending, never as inactive.

  • Late steps put things right

    If steps for a past day turn up later, Evochi recalculates and gives back any vitality it took.

  • A pet, not a doctor

    Your Evochi's mood is a game state. It never judges your health, and Evochi never reads heart rate.

  • One quiet reminder, at most

    Reminders are off until you turn them on: one nudge a day, outside your quiet hours.

Privacy

Your steps, and nothing else

Evochi asks Apple Health or Health Connect for one thing: your step count. In practice that means:

  • Read-only. Evochi never writes anything to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Steps only. No heart rate, sleep, location, contacts or photos.
  • Daily totals. Detailed step samples are folded into daily totals after 14 days.
  • Never sold, never for ads. Your step data isn't sold or used for advertising.
  • Opt-in usage stats. Anonymous product events are sent only if you switch them on, and never include health data.
  • Yours to take or delete. Export or delete everything from Settings → Privacy & data.

FAQ

Questions, answered

{{faqBeta}}
Is Evochi free?

Evochi is free to download. Everything in the Evochi Market is bought with EvoCoins, which you earn by walking and completing quests.

Do I need a smartwatch or fitness tracker?

No. Your phone already counts steps, and Evochi reads that count from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. If a watch records steps too, Evochi merges the overlapping time so nothing is counted twice.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone, using the Health app, and Android phones with Health Connect. Health Connect is built into Android 14 and later, and earlier versions can install it from Google Play.

What if I can't walk for a while?

Your Evochi gets two protected rest days in every seven, and a low day only counts after a 48-hour grace period. Over a longer break its vitality drops slowly and it may get sleepy or sick. A good walk or a Healing Herb helps it recover.

If you walk somewhere your phone can't see, like a treadmill, you can add steps by hand.

Can my Evochi die?

Only after a long run of low days, and never by surprise: it gets sleepy and then sick first, and the app shows its vitality all along. If vitality does reach zero, your Evochi rests in the Hall of Memories. You can revive it with three Healing Herbs or by reaching your step goal for a day, or hatch a new egg. You keep every item you own either way.

Will it drain my battery or track my location?

No. Evochi reads step totals your phone already records. It doesn't use GPS or your location.

Can I enter steps by hand?

Yes. Manual steps earn XP and help vitality. They don't unlock chests or cosmetic rewards, which keeps quest rewards fair.

I travel a lot. When does my day start?

Your day follows your account time zone, which you set in Settings. Travelling doesn't move it unless you change it, so a long flight won't cost you a day.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app: see Delete your account.

Is Evochi a fitness or medical app?

No, it's a game. It doesn't diagnose or measure anything about your health, and an Evochi getting sick is a pet state based on game rules. If you're starting a new exercise routine, check with your doctor first.
