How it works

Walk. Grow. Evolve.

Your Evochi grows from the steps your phone records anyway. There's nothing to log and no workout to plan.

  1. Hatch your egg

    Pick one of six colourways, name your Eggling and choose a daily step goal that suits you.

  2. Walk, and it grows

    Steps become XP, with bonuses as you pass milestones through the day and a little more for going past your goal.

  3. Evolve together

    Once it has grown enough, your Eggling can become a Juvenile, and one day an Elder. You choose the moment.

Gentle by design

Rewards for walking. No punishment for resting.

These rules are built into the game, not left to chance.

  • Rest days are protected

    A couple of low-step days in any week never cost your Evochi anything.

  • Time to sync

    Watch data can arrive late, so a low day only counts once your steps have had time to come in.

  • No data isn't a lazy day

    If your phone didn't record steps, the day shows as pending, never as inactive.

  • Never harmed on adventures

    In the wilds your Evochi can be knocked back, never hurt. Step away whenever you like.

  • A pet, not a doctor

    Your Evochi's mood is a game state. It never judges your health, and Evochi never reads heart rate.

  • One quiet reminder, at most

    Reminders are off until you turn them on: one nudge a day, outside your quiet hours.

Privacy

Your steps, and nothing else

Evochi asks Apple Health or Health Connect for one thing: your step count. In practice that means:

  • Read-only. Evochi never writes anything to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Steps only. No heart rate, sleep, location, contacts or photos.
  • Never sold, never for ads. Your step data isn't sold or used for advertising.
  • Opt-in usage stats. Anonymous product events are sent only if you switch them on, and never include health data.
  • Yours to take or delete. Export or delete everything from Settings → Privacy & data.

FAQ

Questions, answered

{{faqBeta}}
Is Evochi free?

Evochi is free to download and play. Coins for the Market are earned by walking and playing.

There are optional in-app purchases: Glimmer (a premium currency), a Season Pass and an Evochi Plus membership. Cosmetics bought with real money only change how your Evochi looks, and Evochi never sells random rewards for real money.

Do I need a smartwatch or fitness tracker?

No. Your phone already counts steps, and Evochi reads that count from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. If a watch records steps too, Evochi merges the overlapping time so nothing is counted twice.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone, using the Health app, and Android phones with Health Connect. Health Connect is built into Android 14 and later, and earlier versions can install it from Google Play.

Can I play on a day I didn't walk?

Yes. Walking is what makes your Evochi grow, but caring for it, the village, your garden and the trails' adventures are there on rest days too.

What if I can't walk for a while?

Rest days are protected, and a low day only counts once your steps have had time to sync. Over a longer break your Evochi may get sleepy or unwell, never by surprise, and a good walk or a Healing Herb helps it recover.

If you walk somewhere your phone can't see, like a treadmill, you can add steps by hand.

Can my Evochi die?

Only after a long run of low days, and never by surprise: it gets sleepy and then unwell first, and the app shows how it's doing all along. If it does fade, it rests in the Hall of Memories, where you can bring it back or hatch a new egg. You keep everything you own either way.

Will it drain my battery or track my location?

No. Evochi reads step totals your phone already records. It doesn't use GPS or your location.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app: see Delete your account.

Is Evochi a fitness or medical app?

No, it's a game. It doesn't diagnose or measure anything about your health, and an Evochi feeling unwell is a pet state based on game rules. If you're starting a new exercise routine, check with your doctor first.
