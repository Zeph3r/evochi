The short version

  • With your permission, Evochi reads one thing from Apple Health or Health Connect: your step count. It never writes health data and never reads heart rate, sleep, location or anything else.
  • We keep daily step totals and your game progress. Detailed step samples are folded into daily totals after 14 days.
  • We don't sell your data, and we never use step data for advertising.
  • Anonymous usage events are off unless you switch them on, and never include health data.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time from Settings → Privacy & data.

How we use it

  • To run the game: turning steps into XP, EvoCoins and vitality, unlocking evolutions and keeping quests and your wallet accurate.
  • To keep things fair: matching step samples from your phone and watch so nothing is counted twice, and recording each coin transaction once so rewards can't be claimed twice.
  • To sync your progress across devices if you have an account.
  • To help you when you contact support.
  • To improve Evochi using usage events, only if you've turned them on.
  • To meet legal obligations and keep the service secure.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build advertising profiles, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

We rely on contract to provide the game you asked for; on your explicit consent for step data, which you give through your phone's Health permission screen and can withdraw at any time; on consent for usage events; on our legitimate interests for security and fraud prevention; and on legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Step data from Apple Health and Health Connect

Step data is health-related, so we hold ourselves to stricter rules for it:

  • We ask for read access to step count only: "Steps" in Apple Health, and the "Steps" permission in Health Connect. We never ask to write data.
  • We use step data only to provide Evochi's gameplay.
  • We don't sell step data, use it for advertising or marketing, or share it with data brokers or information resellers.
  • We don't use step data to decide anyone's eligibility for credit, insurance, employment or similar.
  • We don't store step data in iCloud.
  • People at {{companyName}} don't look at your step data, unless you ask us to while we help you, it's needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.
  • We only pass step data to service providers who store or process it for us to run Evochi, under contracts that keep it confidential.

Evochi's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop Evochi reading steps at any time. iPhone: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → Evochi, and turn off Steps. Android: open Health Connect, then App permissions → Evochi. Evochi keeps working, and you can add steps by hand.

Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Export: Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data gives you a copy of your game data and step history.
  • Delete: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data removes everything. You can also ask without the app on our account deletion page.
  • Usage events: switch Share anonymous usage events on or off whenever you like.
  • Step access: turn it off in Apple Health or Health Connect, as described above.
  • Reminders: off unless you turn them on, and quiet hours apply.
