VO is optional on every cut; the burned-in captions carry the message. Duck the music ~6 dB under the voice.

How to run it
====================================================================
Week 1: video 1 (dev diary) + video 2 (pick your egg). These build the audience and the comments.
Week 2: video 3 (crown). Scarcity works once people already want the thing.
Week 3+: video 4 (hatch goal), once the counter is live and past ~1,000, so it looks reachable.
Re-post video 1 with the alternative hooks below as new "days" of the diary.

Alternative first lines to test on video 1:
  "I quit my job to make a walking pet game." (only if true)
  "POV: a lizard egg is the reason you walked today."
  "day 1 of building the cosiest walking game on your phone."

Waitlist page checklist (the videos promise these, so the page has to deliver them):
  - Email + "pick your egg" colour at sign-up (reserve the colour; show it on the thank-you screen).
  - Position in line after signing up, and a share link that moves both people up (referral).
  - A live counter toward 5,000 with the egg getting brighter as it fills.
  - "Founder's Crown: first 1,000" with the real count of spots left.
  - Privacy line: email only, used for the beta invite, delete on request.
